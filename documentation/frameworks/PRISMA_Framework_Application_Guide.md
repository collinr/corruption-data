## 2. Why PRISMA Applies to this Study

### **This Research is a Systematic Data Source Mapping**

PRISMA was originally designed for systematic reviews of research studies (quantitative, qualitative, mixed-methods). However, your this study is conceptually similar:

**Parallel structure:**

| Systematic Review of Studies | Multi-Source Data Mapping |
|---|---|
| Search for published studies | Identify publicly available corruption data sources |
| Define inclusion/exclusion criteria | Define scope: government, NGO, civil society |
| Appraise quality of each study | Evaluate each data source (credibility, completeness, accessibility) |
| Extract data from studies | Extract relevant data from within sources |
| Synthesize findings across studies | Triangulate findings across sources |
| Report using PRISMA checklist | Document methodology transparently |

**Key insight:** I am not aggregating research *findings*; I am cataloguing and evaluating *data sources* as artifacts. PRISMA's systematic methodology applies equally.

### **Phase 2: SCREENING**

**Objective:** Apply inclusion/exclusion criteria to identify eligible sources.

#### **Step 2.1: Define Inclusion Criteria (a priori)**

**Include sources if:**

1. **Publicly accessible** (or accessible via ethics approval for restricted data like whistleblower databases)
2. **Legitimate producer:** Government institution, NGO with governance focus, or established civil society organisation
3. **Contains relevant data:** Quantitative or structured qualitative data on corruption, fraud, waste, or governance failures
4. **South Africa focus:** Data on SA institutions, municipalities, or national systems
5. **Documented scope:** Source clearly describes what is included (not speculation/rumour)
6. **Time span:** Covers at least 3+ years of data (or clear explanation why shorter)

**Exclude sources if:**

1. **Anecdotal only:** No underlying institutional data (e.g., "my friend said...")
2. **Permanently restricted:** Cannot access even with ethics approval (classified national security)
3. **Opinion without evidence:** Commentary with no supporting data (e.g., political opinion pieces)
4. **Duplicate:** Already catalogued; reference instead of double-counting
5. **Non-SA focused:** International examples or comparative data without SA relevance
6. **Inactive:** Archive/historical with no updates; no new data added >3 years

#### **Step 2.2: Two-Stage Screening Process**

**Stage 1: Title/Abstract Screening (Surface-level)**

For each identified source, quickly determine relevance:

| Source Name | Type | Inclusion Criterion Met? | Reason | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AGSA PFMA Reports 2025 | Audit | Yes (criteria 2,3,4,5,6) | Institutional audit, national scope, quantitative | **INCLUDE** |
| AGSA MFMA Reports 2025 | Audit | Yes (criteria 2,3,4,5,6) | Institutional audit, municipal scope, quantitative | **INCLUDE** |
| Zondo Commission Report | Inquiry | Yes (criteria 2,3,4,5) | Government inquiry, narrative + evidence, SA scope | **INCLUDE** |
| Anonymous blog "Corruption Stories" | Blog | No (criteria 1,2,5) | Anecdotal, unverified, no institutional backing | **EXCLUDE** |
| Transparency Int'l CPI 2025         | Index | Yes (criteria 2,3,4,6) | Established NGO, governance metric, SA included | **INCLUDE** |
| Personal Twitter thread on SARS     | Social media | No (criteria 5,6) | Anecdotal, not institutional, single event | **EXCLUDE** |

**Output:** Maintain a screening log with decisions and reasons (for reproducibility & audit trail).

**Stage 2: Full-Text Screening (Deep-level)**

For sources that passed Stage 1, retrieve full documentation and evaluate:
- Actual coverage (does it contain what the title promises?)
- Data quality (documented methodology, definitions, limitations)
- Accessibility (can you actually access/download the data?)
- Completeness (enough detail to extract/analyse?)

### **Phase 3: ELIGIBILITY ASSESSMENT**

**Objective:** Detailed evaluation of each source against your specific quality criteria.

#### **Step 3.1: Apply Quality Assessment Framework (Table 4 from Inventory)**

For each included source, score across seven dimensions:

**Quality Assessment Tool:**

| Source | Credibility (1–5) | Completeness (1–5) | Consistency (1–5) | Timeliness (1–5) | Accessibility (1–5) | Granularity (1–5) | Bias Risk (1–5) | **Composite Score** | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AGSA PFMA 2025 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 | **4.1** | Excellent credibility; 12-month lag; definitions align with regulatory framework |
| Corruption Watch | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 3 | 2 | **3.6** | High accessibility; crowdsourced bias (media coverage skew); good timeliness |
| CPI 2025 | 5 | 1 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 1 | 3 | **3.4** | Perception-based, not empirical; composite index obscures underlying data |
| Social media (Twitter) | 2 | 1 | 1 | 5 | 5 | 2 | 1 | **2.4** | Real-time; severe bias (urban, English, bot noise); unverifiable allegations |

**Scoring definitions (from Data Evaluation Matrix in proposal):**

| Dimension | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Credibility** | Independent audit, legislated mandate, peer-reviewed | Established institution, transparent methods | Mixed: some standards, some gaps | Weak credentials, limited transparency | Unverified, no institutional backing |
| **Completeness** | Comprehensive coverage (all cases/sectors) | Broad coverage, minor gaps | Selective coverage (main cases) | Limited scope | Anecdotal, fragments only |
| **Consistency** | Standardised definitions, aligned across years | Mostly consistent, minor variations | Moderate variation in definitions | Inconsistent reporting | No consistent framework |
| **Timeliness** | Real-time or <3 months lag | 3–6 months lag | 6–12 months lag | 1–2 years lag | >2 years lag |
| **Accessibility** | Open download, API, no paywalls | Publicly available, minimal friction | Semi-public, some access restrictions | Restricted, requires request | Confidential, blocked |
| **Granularity** | Case-level detail, person-level, linkable IDs | Sector/institutional level, disaggregable | Departmental aggregates | Regional/provincial only | Country-level only |
| **Bias Risk (reverse: 1=high, 5=low)** | Low bias, representative sampling | Minor known biases, documented | Moderate bias, acknowledged | High bias, potential distortion | Severe bias, unreliable |

#### **Step 3.2: Determine Inclusion Based on Quality**

**Minimum quality threshold:**
- **Composite score ≥3.5:** Include in primary analysis (reliable sources)
- **Composite score 2.5–3.4:** Include in secondary analysis (supplementary sources, acknowledge limitations)
- **Composite score <2.5:** Mention in limitations section; do not use for primary findings

**Rationale:** Prevents over-reliance on low-credibility sources while maintaining transparency.

### **Phase 4: DATA EXTRACTION**

**Objective:** Systematically extract relevant data *within* each included source.

#### **Step 4.1: Define Data Items to Extract**

For each source, extract standardized information:

**Source-level data (metadata):**
- Institution name & type (government/NGO/civil society)
- Data source name & description
- URL/access point
- Timeline (start year–end year)
- Data type (quantitative, qualitative, mixed)
- Format (PDF, Excel, API, etc.)
- Machine-readable? (yes/no)
- Licensing terms (open, restricted, requires request)
- Last updated

**Corruption-level data (from within sources):**
- Corruption type (fraud, waste, irregular expenditure, state capture, procurement irregularities, etc.)
- Sector affected (health, education, infrastructure, police, etc.)
- Jurisdiction (national/provincial/municipal/specific department)
- Date of incident
- Rand value (if quantified)
- Outcome (investigated/prosecuted/recovered/pending)
- Source credibility (did multiple sources report this case?)

## 6. Integration with Existing Framework

### **PRISMA + Bowen Document Analysis + Data Evaluation Matrix**

These three frameworks are **complementary, not contradictory:**

| Framework | Purpose | Your Use |
|---|---|---|
| **PRISMA** | Systematic identification, screening, selection of sources | *How to systematically find and validate sources* |
| **Bowen (Document Analysis)** | Rigorous analysis of document content; trustworthiness criteria | *How to appraise and extract data from sources* |
| **Data Evaluation Matrix (Table 4)** | Seven-dimension quality scoring | *Specific operationalization of Bowen's criteria + PRISMA quality appraisal* |

**Integration workflow:**

1. **PRISMA:** Identify & screen sources systematically (Phase 1–3)
2. **Matrix:** Evaluate quality of screened sources (Phase 3 → Table 4 scoring)
3. **Bowen:** Extract data from high-quality sources using document analysis principles (Phase 4)
4. **Triangulation:** Synthesize across sources (Phase 5)
