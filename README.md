## Study Overview

### Research Questions

1. What publicly available data sources exist on corruption and governance in South Africa?
2. How do data sources differ in structure, quality, and scope?
3. What does the data tell us about corruption and governance in South Africa?

### Methodological Approach

This research employs:
- **PRISMA-ScR** for systematic source identification and screening
- **Bowen's document analysis** for rigorous appraisal of source quality
- **Research onion framework** for coherent methodological design
- **Mixed methods** combining qualitative characterization (80%) with quantitative metrics (20%)
- **Triangulation analysis** to identify convergence and gaps across sources

### Key Output

A comprehensive, replicable framework for:
- Systematically mapping corruption data sources
- Evaluating source quality and accessibility
- Identifying data gaps and fragmentation points
- Supporting evidence-based anti-corruption policy
- Enhancing data literacy in the public sector
