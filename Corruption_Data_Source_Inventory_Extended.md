|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Structured (CSV/API)** | Vulekamali, Veza, Afrobarometer, WBGI | Automated download + parsing | Checksum verification, row counts | 1–2 | Low (0–5%) |

## Table 4: Quality & Credibility Assessment Framework

**For each source, assess against the following dimensions:**

| **Dimension** | **Scale** | **Definition** | **Exemplar (High=5)** | **Exemplar (Low=1)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Credibility** | 1–5 | Producer mandate, methodology transparency, peer review, independence | AGSA (legislated mandate, audit standards, peer reviewed) | Social media rumours, unverified allegations |
| **Completeness** | 1–5 | Coverage of scope (how many corruption cases/sectors are included?) | AGSA (all departments annually) | Corruption Watch (selected high-profile cases) |
| **Consistency** | 1–5 | Stable definitions, standardised reporting over time | Vulekamali (machine-readable, consistent schema) | PFMA self-reported irregular expenditure (varying definitions by department) |
| **Timeliness** | 1–5 | Lag between event & publication; frequency of updates | Veza (real-time API updates) | Zondo Commission (10-year retrospective, published 2022–2024) |
| **Accessibility** | 1–5 | Public availability, licensing, no paywalls or deliberate barriers | Afrobarometer (open CSV download) | Hawks investigations (restricted; selective press releases) |
| **Granularity** | 1–5 | Depth of detail (case-level, person-level, institutional, sectoral) | Court judgements (specific facts, evidence, sentencing) | World Bank indices (country-level aggregates only) |
| **Bias Risk** | 1–5 | Known biases in collection, reporting, or coverage (reverse score: 1=high bias, 5=low bias) | Parliamentary Q&A (bias toward government priorities) | Social media (urban, English-language, bot-driven bias) |

## Table 5: Integration & Triangulation Map

**How to use multiple sources to corroborate findings:**

| **Corruption Type** | **Primary Source** | **Secondary Source** | **Tertiary Source** | **Convergence Indicator** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Procurement Fraud** | AGSA audit findings | Vulekamali contract data | Media investigations + OUTA reports | Same case named across ≥2 sources; similar Rand values |
| **State Capture (Systemic)** | Zondo Commission narratives | SIU case summaries + NPA convictions | Journalism archives + Civil society reporting | Named officials, entities, timelines align |
| **Irregular Expenditure** | PFMA department self-reports | AGSA audit confirmations | Parliamentary oversight findings | Quantitative alignment; acknowledgement in multiple sources |
| **Police Misconduct / Corruption** | IPID annual reports | Veza case-level data | Afrobarometer citizen experience surveys | IPID case count ≈ Veza records; survey corroborates prevalence |
| **Whistleblower Suppression** | Public Protector maladministration findings | Whistleblower protection org reports | News coverage of threats/retaliation | Timeline and narrative alignment across sources |
| **Asset Recovery Delays** | Asset Forfeiture Unit reports | NPA conviction outcomes + sentencing | Court case records | Timeline: conviction → asset order → recovery (lagging by 2–5 years) |
| **Citizen Perception vs Reality** | Afrobarometer + CPI surveys | Corruption Watch incidents + AGSA audit frequency | Vulekamali spending transparency | Perception inflation/deflation: sectors perceived corrupt vs. statistically documented |

## Methodological Notes for Your Study

### **1. Selection & Inclusion Criteria**

**Include if:**
- Source is publicly accessible (or accessible under ethical approval for restricted data)
- Contains quantitative or structured qualitative data related to governance, audit outcomes, public procurement, financial misconduct, irregular/fruitless/wasteful expenditure, or corruption perception/experience
- Produced by legitimate government institution, CSO, or anti-corruption NGO
- Data covers South Africa (national, provincial, or municipal level)

**Exclude if:**
- Source is anecdotal or hearsay without institutional backing
- Access is permanently restricted (e.g., classified national security documents)
- Source is opinion-only with no underlying evidence (e.g., political commentary without data)
- Data is duplicative of an already-catalogued source (reference cross-linkage instead)

### **2. Data Extraction Workflow**

1. **Inventory stage**: Populate Table 1 columns (institution, source, content, timeline, format, accessibility)
2. **Appraisal stage**: Evaluate each source against Table 4 criteria; score 1–5 per dimension; calculate composite credibility score
3. **Extraction stage**: Use Table 3 typology to select extraction method; document method, tool, QA process
4. **Coding stage**: Apply predefined codebook to extracted data (e.g., "corruption type," "sector," "outcome," "Rand value")
5. **Synthesis stage**: Use Table 5 triangulation map to identify convergence across sources; flag contradictions

### **3. Key Tensions to Document**

- **Perception vs. Reality**: CPI and Afrobarometer measure *perceptions*; AGSA and SIU measure *documented cases*. These may not correlate. Explain the gap.
- **Completeness vs. Accessibility**: AGSA is comprehensive but PDFs require manual extraction. Vulekamali is machine-readable but covers only National Treasury spending.
- **Time lag**: Zondo Commission is authoritative but retrospective (2009–2018). Current corruption rates may differ. Acknowledge the temporal mismatch.
- **Definition variability**: "Irregular expenditure" is defined differently by AGSA vs. MFMA vs. individual departments. Harmonise or flag.
- **Coverage bias**: Media and civil society sources over-represent high-profile cases. Low-level corruption (junior official taking a bribe) is under-reported. Acknowledge in limitations.

### **4. Recommended Visualisations**

- **Heatmap**: Source × Evaluation Criteria (colour intensity = score). Shows which sources are strong on credibility vs. timeliness vs. accessibility.
- **Timeline chart**: Sources arranged by time span (start year, end year, currency). Reveals data gaps and overlaps.
- **Sankey diagram**: Flow from source → sector → corruption type. Shows which sources illuminate which corruption pathways.
- **Venn diagram**: Overlap of cases between AGSA, SIU, media, and civil society. Identifies corroborated vs. single-source allegations.
- **Institution network graph**: Nodes = sources; edges = cross-citations or shared data. Shows ecosystem connectedness and fragmentation.
